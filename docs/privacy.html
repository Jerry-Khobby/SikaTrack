SikaTrack

Privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026

The short version: SikaTrack reads your MoMo messages and nothing else, uses them only to show you your own money, and only you see what it builds from them. We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking tools.

What we collect, and why

Your MoMo SMS

With your permission, SikaTrack reads SMS from MTN MoMo and GhanaPay only. The app picks them out on your phone, so every other message stays there and is never uploaded. The MoMo SMS are sent to SikaTrack's server, where they're turned into your transactions: amounts, fees, balances, who you paid and categories.

Your account

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's ID and your email address (only if Google has verified it). We also keep the name you choose in the app, your settings, your budgets, the wallets you add, and any category or name changes you make to your transactions.

Your phone

A random ID the app creates for your phone, its model and the app's version, so you can stay signed in and the app can be supported. If you turn on notifications, a notification token for this phone.

Who can see it

Only you, in the app. Other people's phone numbers in your SMS are never shown; people you pay appear by name. We don't sell or share your data with anyone, and there are no ads or analytics trackers.

A few services are needed to run SikaTrack:

  • Google, to sign you in.
  • Our hosting provider, whose server stores and processes your data for us.
  • Expo, to deliver notifications, when you've turned them on.

We'd only disclose data if the law required it.

How it's protected

Everything between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). Your sign-in is kept in your phone's secure storage. The database and file storage aren't reachable from the internet, only through the SikaTrack server, and our logs never contain your SMS text or phone numbers.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in the app under More → Delete account. You're signed out on every device and your data is hidden at once. After 7 days, everything is erased for good: your SMS, your transactions, your account and your settings. Signing in again within those 7 days cancels the deletion.

Removing a wallet keeps its past transactions; deleting your account removes everything.

Permissions the app asks for

  • SMS (read): to read your MoMo messages, as above. You can turn it off any time in Android's settings; SikaTrack then stops reading new messages.
  • Notifications: optional, for budget and low-balance alerts.

Children

SikaTrack isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date, and tell you in the app if the change matters. Questions or requests about your data: jerrymardeburg@gmail.com.
