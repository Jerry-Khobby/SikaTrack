How to install

SikaTrack isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android asks you to confirm a few things the first time.

  1. Download the APK with the button above.
  2. Open it from your notifications or your Downloads folder.
  3. If Android says it can't install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source for your browser, and go back.
  4. If Google Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details, then Install anyway. It warns about every app from outside the Play Store.
  5. Tap Install, then Open, and sign in with your Google account.

Letting SikaTrack read your MoMo SMS

On Android 13 and newer, apps installed from a download can't get the SMS permission until you allow it once. If SikaTrack's SMS step says the setting is restricted:

  1. Open Settings → Apps → SikaTrack.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right, then Allow restricted settings, and confirm with your PIN or fingerprint.
  3. Go back to SikaTrack and tap Allow access again.
SikaTrack only reads SMS from MTN MoMo and GhanaPay. Every other message stays on your phone and is never uploaded. How we handle your data.
